# Deformations by lines and shapes

The form page with a `deform()` step on the real Anny body that moves the joints with it: line of action (C, S, front and side), squash and stretch with volume kept, straight-against-curved limbs, limbs as arcs, taper, contrapposto, the whole body as one shape (rectangle, trapezoid, triangle, V, hourglass, pear, egg, diamond, bean). Uses `../data`. Pictures: [docs/pictures/lines](../../docs/pictures/lines/).
