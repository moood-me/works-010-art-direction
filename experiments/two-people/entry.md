# For the orchestrator — К2

**Name:** Двое: касание, рукопожатие, за руку, перенос — через ограничения Kimodo (two people: a touch, a handshake,
leading by the hand, a carry — through Kimodo's constraints).

**What it is:** four scenes of two people where every motion is Kimodo's, made one person at a time in the order of
cause and effect — a place on one person's body (our body, our proportions) becomes the other one's hand constraint in
Kimodo's own space; reactions go on with `after`. A man lays his hand on a woman's shoulder and keeps it there as she
looks round; two people walk up and shake hands (he made first, her palm constrained to his); a man walks leading a
woman by the hand (he first, her palm on his every 10 frames); a man lifts a child to his chest, carries it and puts it
down (the child attached to his hands, its own hands constrained onto his shoulders). Page:
`experiments/two-people/index.html` (the newest versions first, the earlier ones beside them).

**Verdict:** works in all four by the principle Pavel described. Kimodo puts a hand within 2–7 cm of its place on
average (more between keyframes); a small correction on top holds the palm on it every frame. Both-moving scenes came
out on the first or second try. Weak: hands are flat (no grip) and overlap 2–3 cm where fingers would wrap; in the touch
his look at her is a correction on top; in leading her gait leans forward; in the carry the child's legs hang straight
and its foot meets his shin as he walks (two tries to give the legs through the pose made it worse — left), and for a
second at the set-down their hands go into each other.
