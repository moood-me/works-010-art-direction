# Heads from 3D-cartoon references

Eleven heads after Pavel's 3D-cartoon references, as studies, on one shared template: a smooth head of a few masses
(cranium, face, chin, cheeks, nose, ears, neck) with a ray mesh in rings around the face; eyes as balls with a painted
iris seen only in the lid opening (almond, tilted, a soft upper-lid fold, a winged lash line); brows and mouth as
strokes on the skin; hair as a cap sinking below the hairline plus wide locks along curves, braid, buns, ponytail, a
streak, a helmet; blink, brows up, angry brows, smile. Anny's body in a natural pose with her head hidden.
Pictures: [docs/pictures/heads-3d](../../docs/pictures/heads-3d/).

**Second round** (after Pavel's notes, staying with sculpting by volumes): the head as a cranium egg plus a face mass
cut by planes (cheek to jaw angle, jaw to chin, under-chin to throat, face below the brows), each edge with its own
softness per character; a column neck; noses as wedges with a ridge, ball tip, wings and carved nostrils; added
Giulia, Ambrosius, Nimona. Sheets in `docs/pictures/heads-3d/v2`.

**Third round, "every face form exact":** the cranium egg had eaten the face (same fault as in heads-anime) — its lower
front removed; the face mass a rounded block cut by planes; widths at cheekbones and jaw and chin projection measured
against each character's numbers; plane bounds that left shelves and grooves removed; the cheekbone ridge softened;
eye-socket cuts off. Sheets in `docs/pictures/heads-3d/v3`.
