# Heads from anime and drawn references

Nine heads after Pavel's anime and drawn references, as studies. Anny's body and garments posed on the SOMA skeleton,
her head removed; a designed head (a distance field: big cranium, a soft face cone, nose, ears, neck stub) rigid on the
Head joint, shaded as one smooth form; eyes, brows, mouth, blush, mask painted on the face (anime, sharp cel-style,
ringed, smiling eyes); hair as swept ribbons or as a mass with pointed clumps melted into its edge; hoods, a bun; blink,
smile and brows by redrawing the face. `shot.js` shoots, `sheet.py` tiles.
Pictures: [docs/pictures/heads-anime](../../docs/pictures/heads-anime/).

**Second version, after Pavel's note** that proportions weren't conveyed and the faces came out alike: each face is
lofted from its own two curves read off its picture — a front outline (forehead, cheekbone, jaw angle, chin) and a
side profile (forehead, brow, under-eye, philtrum, lips, chin); noses as wedges (bridge root, tip, underside); lips and
cheeks or jowls per character; eyes, brows and mouths drawn per picture; a camera at each picture's angle. Sheets
`sheetE*.png`.

**Third version, after Pavel's note on lines and curves and the neck:** noses as drawn side curves (root, concave,
straight or humped bridge, sharp or soft tip, underside back to the lip, width growing to the nostrils); mouths from
their own parting line, upper lip with a bow, lower lip, gaps; tapered brows with their own arch; lid creases and
extra lines; Anny's neck removed with her head and the designed neck measured to meet her cut ring, the jaw's
underside sloping into the throat. Added Alva and Jesper from "Klaus". Sheets `sheetF*.png`.
