# Другие тела: стол К1 и касание К2 на другом росте

**Folder:** `experiments/other-bodies/` · **page:** `experiments/other-bodies/index.html` · **grew from:** К1
(`experiments/contacts/`), К2 (`experiments/two-people/`).

**What it is.** A test of the catalogue as a next agent meets it: two scenes repeated by К1's and К2's notes and code
alone on bodies they were not made for — the table lean for a child, a heavy man (also at a 1.1 m bar counter) and a
2.31 m body; the hand on the shoulder with the look back for a man and a short woman, and for a child who can reach
only the woman's arm; and the same order with other seeds.

**Verdict.** The way repeats: given the places of this body, Kimodo brings the hands as near as it did for К1's and
К2's own bodies (in its space 2–4.5 cm for the leans, exact for the touches), and the small correction puts the palm
on the place. The man and the short woman read as К2's scene; the child's touch reads after one more try (the first
stance, К2's, put the child against her back). What does not carry over is what the notes treat as the scene's
details: where to stand, which way the hand lies, what the prompt says, and the woman's look — on another body they
are the main work. The heavy man at the table and at the bar reads best of the tables. The 2.31 m body (arms short for its legs) does
not reach the table at all: dividing places by one k assumes SOMA's proportions. The child at a table stoops over it
like an adult in two seeds of three. One seed in three of the touch let the hand wander 21 cm between the keyframes: one take is luck. Where
the notes were not enough is the longest part of `notes.md`.
