# Clothes with their own form

The dressed page (`../dressed`) with `tailor`: a garment is read around an axis (torso along the spine and straight
down below the hips, each sleeve along the arm, each leg along the leg); at each height and angle the radius is taken
and a designed form drawn on those rings. A garment's design: stiffness (cloth bridges the body's hollows), where it
hangs from (shoulders, chest, waist, hips), silhouette (skirts: straight, A, bell, full, tulip, pencil, trapeze,
balloon; sleeves: fitted, straight, puff, bell, bishop, cap; legs: straight, wide, flare, tapered, harem), section
(round to square), hem length, folds as drawing. One knob `cloth`, with the body's simplification: real cloth → drawn
form with folds → smooth form → sign (a cone from chest or shoulders, plain tubes).
Pictures: [docs/pictures/clothes](../../docs/pictures/clothes/).

**On other people and in motion:** ladders on men, children, old people (`men.png`, `kids.png`); dresses through walk,
jump and sitting compared three ways — the form recomputed around the axes each frame, the form glued to the skin, and
cloth physics with the designed form as its rest shape carried by the pelvis (`mo.js`, modes `__phys` / `__ride`).
