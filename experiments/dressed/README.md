# Dressed, with a face

The form page with Anny's MakeHuman garments and hair fitted to the styled body, modelled eyes (ball, socket, iris,
lid, lash line, glint), brush-stroke brows and mouth, a "drawn face" pass that melts realistic forms into one soft
surface, and a soft nose bump. `gen.py` generates random characters across body styles, character types, sexes and
ages, hair forms, outfits, palettes and every face setting; three generations of 24, each keeping what worked.
Garments and hair come from `../anny/data`, bodies from `../form/data`.
