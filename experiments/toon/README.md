# Expressive faces and rubber-hose limbs

Eleven original toon characters on moood's 77-joint SOMA skeleton (`joints.json`, from `engines/1/bodies`), each with
its own bone lengths; geometry rebuilt from joint positions only, so motion can drive it.
- limbs as one arc through shoulder, elbow and wrist — bend 0 (broken sticks), 1 (arc), 1.8 (noodle);
- the face drawn on a canvas over the head: many eye and pupil shapes, lids tilted by mood, wedge brows, mouths (grins
  with sharp, square or buck teeth, smirk, wobble, O, pout), extras; nine expressions on any head;
- shapes at the limit: a torso lofted along the spine, ribbed and muzzled heads, horns, ears, tails, hats, gloves with
  fat curled fingers, claws, hooves; toon with outline or soft plastic.
`shot.js` renders sheets, `cast.json` holds the characters. Pictures: [docs/pictures/toon](../../docs/pictures/toon/).
