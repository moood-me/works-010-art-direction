# Вещи в руках: дверь, чашка, коробка

**Folder:** `experiments/moving-things/` · **page:** `experiments/moving-things/index.html` · **grew from:** К1
(`experiments/contacts/`) and the early engine (`engine/`, Д1) with its Anny bodies.

**What it is.** К1's way for things that move with the hand. A woman opens a door by its lever and walks through; a
woman takes a cup from a table, drinks and puts it back; a man takes a box from the floor in both hands, carries it
3 m and puts it on a table. Every place the hand must come to — the handle along its arc, the cup where it stands and
at the lips, the box's sides on the floor and on the table — is given to Kimodo as its own hand constraint; Kimodo
makes the walk, the reach, the drink, the squat and the carry; a small arm correction finishes the touch on our body;
the thing follows the hand while it is held (the door about its hinge).

**Verdict.** The principle carries over to moving things: all three read as what they are. The box is the best —
Kimodo does it almost by itself, the box comes down exactly on its place. The door works after three takes (a walk,
then the door; the hand hooked over the lever; held till the hand leaves it, the door swinging on). The cup works
after three takes (Kimodo drinks seated; the drink had to be given as the cup's rim at the lips); at the mouth the
correction is large (~10 cm). Weak everywhere: no grip — the hand lies on the thing, the fingers go 1–3 cm into it.
Pushing or pulling along the floor was not tried.
