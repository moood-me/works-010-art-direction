# Вещи в руках: дверь, чашка, коробка, стул

**Folder:** `experiments/moving-things/` · **page:** `experiments/moving-things/index.html` · **grew from:** К1
(`experiments/contacts/`) and the early engine (`engine/`, Д1) with its Anny bodies.

**What it is.** К1's way for things that move with the hand. A woman opens a door by its lever and walks through; a
woman takes a cup from a table, drinks and puts it back; a man takes a box from the floor in both hands, carries it
3 m and puts it on a table; a woman pulls a chair out from a table by its back. Every place the hand must come to — the handle along its arc, the cup where it stands and
at the lips, the box's sides on the floor and on the table — is given to Kimodo as its own hand constraint; Kimodo
makes the walk, the reach, the drink, the squat and the carry; a small arm correction finishes the touch on our body;
the thing follows the hand while it is held (the door about its hinge).

**Verdict.** The principle carries over to moving things: all four read as what they are. While a thing is held the
fingers close round it (Kimodo gives none; our body has them — a simple curl per thing, faded in and out): round the
mug, over the lever, over the chair's back. The mug sits in the fist, upright while carried, tilted at the lips. The
box is Kimodo's best — it comes down exactly on its place; he now stands and bends to put it down (the scene ends
before Kimodo crouches after letting go). The door took three takes, the cup three (Kimodo drinks seated; the drink
was given as the rim at the lips; there the correction is large, ~10 cm). The chair came out first time. Weak: the
grip is a fixed curl, not fitted to the thing; on the box the palms press flat. The first pass's pictures are in
`pics/v1/`.
