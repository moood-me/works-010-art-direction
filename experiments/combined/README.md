# Directions A and B combined

The drawn bodies of direction A with B's clothes; each character's head built around its eyes from a few numbers
(big vault, small low face narrowing to the chin, round cheeks, small nose) with Anny's head laid onto it along rays;
the eye as an opening in the skin (lids are the face's own skin over the ball, almond cut, iris, pupil, glint, a
painted lash line); brows, lash line and mouth painted on the skin; one-sided expressions. Eight characters.
