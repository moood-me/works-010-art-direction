# Anny's head wrapped onto designed heads

Anny's head mesh wrapped onto the third-version designed heads from `../heads-anime`: landmarks matched, the midline
laid point by point along the drawing's profile, non-rigid fit with smoothness, folds found (triangles facing away
from the drawn surface) and eased back; the head stays part of Anny's mesh, so the neck has no seam. The face moves as
mesh: lids laid over the eyeball for a blink, the lip seam pinned along the drawn mouth with the jaw turned by Anny's
jaw weights, a smile lifting corners and cheeks.
