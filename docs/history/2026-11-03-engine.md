# 2026-11-03 — the engine's foundation (stage 0)

**What called for it.** After the breadth, Pavel and I agreed on the engine view ([directions.md](../directions.md),
"The engine view"; [requirements.md](../requirements.md), "The engine", "Motion"): an engine of few assumptions,
presets as code, built so any aspect can be improved for long without breaking characters, beside the experiments,
not in their place. The plan is in stages, each by its own agent: 0 the foundation, 1 bodies, 2 heads, 3 hair,
4 clothes, 5 characters not from Anny. This is stage 0: the common pieces the rest stands on. Things it had to fix:
every experiment reimplemented skinning; the gallery's shared Skinner (`gallery/player-core.js`) dropped meshes' own
transforms; the player kept the pelvis at its rest x, z, so walking and running played on the spot; `turns()` turned
each bone from the body's own rest direction to SOMA's, undoing designs made at rest (Т5's line of action, limb arcs);
Т8's bodies had no real joints (their viewer guessed them from skin displacement).

**What was done** (all in [engine/](../../engine/README.md); the README has the architecture, formats and how to add):
- *One body description* (`src/body.js`, packs: a JSON header and a binary of arrays): positions, triangles, SOMA's 77
  rest joints (places and frames), skin weights, per-part weights, measures. From moood's 7 baked bodies
  (`tools/bodies_moood.py`, read from `experiments/data`) and Т8's 24 bodies (`tools/bodies_anny.py`).
- *Т8's joints the right way*: Anny's own SOMA joint regression (`sparse_rbf_matrix`: joints as fixed combinations of
  SOMA-topology vertices) carried onto moood's mesh once, through Anny's SOMA surface of the fitted woman laid on
  moood's woman; each body's joints are then her joints moved by the regression of how its own surface moved — the
  head of another age and the smoothing included. Frames turn with their bones. Checked against Anny's exact joints for
  bodies of Anny's settings alone (`--check`): 16–35 mm mean apart (about what Anny's own two topologies differ by
  there), the old guess 35–60 mm.
- *One retargeting* (`src/retarget.js`): found that the bodies' joint frames are SOMA's (a child joint in the same
  direction of its parent's frame within a few degrees), so Kimodo's world rotations are carried frame to frame
  (`Q = q · frameᵀ`) on the body's own bones. The designed rest (joint places) is kept apart from the retargeting
  reference (the frames): nothing measures rest bone directions, so designs ride along in motion. The body travels on
  Kimodo's own root path scaled by hip height; placement anywhere (position, heading); the body set so its lowest
  point is as high as SOMA's skin's lowest (scaled) — on the feet, lying, rolling. Corrections on top
  (`src/corrections.js`: arms off the body, arms out of the head, amplified limbs, damped head and spine), all off
  unless a character asks.
- *One skinning* (`src/skin.js`): centres of rotation (Le & Hodgins 2016) by default — no candy-wrapper at twists (as
  linear blending), no bulge at bends (as dual quaternions), the artists' weights unchanged; dual quaternions and
  linear as options. Т7's two fixes as options: the collarbone takes a share of the arm's drop below rest (Т7: a
  third; a raised arm left to Kimodo's own collarbone — giving it a share of raising too hunched the shoulder), and the
  seam where trunk and collarbone/arm weights meet smoothed (the skin's movement against the chest). Rigid attachments
  take a joint's matrix times their own, keeping their transforms.
- *One motion format and folder* (`engine/motions`, `tools/motions.py`): each file keeps the studio's data unchanged —
  all 77 joints with fingers, rotations and places — with its ask (key, prompts, seconds), `after` and `startsAt` for
  a continuation, and SOMA's lowest skin point per frame. The indie and cloth motions merged: 15 (walk, run, sneak,
  wave, jump, dance, bow, stomp, idle, sit, lie, lying, roll, roll-side, rollover). The studio's rollover had been kept
  only joined to lie (`experiments/cloth/chain.py`, lie cut at 145); its own frames were taken back from there (its
  first five are lie's last five, by what `after` means; joined again, frame 5 lands 4 mm from lie's last).
- *Chains* (`clipOf` in `src/motion.js`): a motion made with `after` plays from its `frames`-th frame after the whole
  previous one, its origin at `startsAt`, heading kept; a chain of motions not made so is refused. lie → rollover is
  the chain shown. No new motions were ordered.
- *Characters* (`characters/*.json`, `src/character.js`): a body id and parameters; versioned — defaults kept per
  version, so a later engine adds a version instead of changing what version 1 means. Ten presets: moood's woman, man,
  child, girl, teenage boy, old woman, old man; Т8's princess with a toddler's head, hero, elf.
- *The picture check* (`check/check.mjs`): every preset in four moments (rest, walk, sit, the chain) rendered and
  compared with `check/pictures/`; tried: a collarbone share changed on one character is caught.
- *The viewer* (`viewer/`), in the gallery as the new group "Д — Движок", Д1: full area beside the menu, the panel
  always there — body, motions and the chain, pause, scrub, the camera following, front/side, joints, skinning methods,
  Т7's fixes, corrections; the floor's metre grid and the pelvis's path. The grid is drawn into the floor's texture: as
  lines, software WebGL dropped those passing behind the camera.

**How, to repeat it.** Python tools make the data (`python3 engine/tools/soma.py`, `bodies_moood.py`, `bodies_anny.py`
— needs Anny from github.com/naver/anny —, `motions.py`). Pictures: `node engine/check/shots.mjs jobs.json outdir`
(headless Chromium, software WebGL, the repository served on a port the system gives); looked at as sheets — motions
front and side on three bodies, travel with a standing camera, the chain around its join, close-ups of shoulders,
elbows and knees with each skinning method. Pictures in [pictures/engine](../pictures/engine/).
