# К1 — contacts: hands come to their places (2026-11-12)

**What called for it.** One of the two tasks Pavel had put off until after the engine (requirements, "After the
engine is finished"): motions that come to the right places through Kimodo's constraints — leaning on a table, a hand
on another's shoulder, an armchair with armrests. On 2026-11-12 he asked to try both with Opus agents, in new
branches from `main`.

**What was done.** An agent made `experiments/contacts/` in the branch `contacts`: three scenes on the early engine
(`engine/`) with moood's Anny bodies. The place is written in our scene, carried into Kimodo's space (divided by our
body's size against Kimodo's) and sent as Kimodo's hand constraint — a whole pose, at two frames. Kimodo makes the
walk, the lean, the sitting and the elbows; a small arm correction on top takes the palm the last 4–6 cm. The first
constrained take was absurd: Kimodo reads rotations against its own T-pose, not the engine's A-pose.

**How it came out.** All three work. The table is the best; the armchair works (the chair is boxes, the knees are
wide); the shoulder lands but is stiff (he stands close, a straight arm). Without constraints Kimodo does none of
them. The orchestrator looked at the sheets and close-ups, not at the films.

**Second tries** (Pavel, the same day: "if you don't like something, or think something could be made better
(simply), or want the agents to try more — ask"). The shoulder was made again the way the two-people agent had found:
he stops beside her, off her shoulder, the elbow bent and hanging, the palm on top of the shoulder away from the neck
— better; he still looks over her (two asks to look at her did not help). The armchair was made again with the whole
pose held at the two frames, the thighs turned in: the knees are together, but Kimodo lifts the hands off the armrests
between those frames, so the correction is twice as large. The first tries are kept beside. The step that keeps a
hand out of the other person's skin now covers the whole arm.

section in the root `index.html` and in README's table.
