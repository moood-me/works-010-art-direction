# 2026-11-12 — four more styles (С17–С20) and one 3D character from each new style (Р45–Р52)

**What called for it.** Pavel asked for three or four more styles unlike the sixteen; I proposed Bilibin and Art
Nouveau, black-and-white seinen manga, low-poly facets and Soviet animation of the 1960s–70s (spares: Silver-Age
American comics, felt and knit toys), and he asked to run four Opus agents on them. Then he asked to turn one or two
random characters of the new styles into 3D the way Р1–Р12 were made, with Fable agents.

**What was done.**
- `styles/s17-bilibin`, `s18-manga`, `s19-lowpoly`, `s20-soviet` (list: `styles/items.json`): each by part B of
  `docs/briefs/styles-more.md` — samples, rules, all six characters (the four and Марат, Кира). Best: С20 (Khitruk's
  people, Марат and Кира), С19 (a line of faceted figurines, Марат), С17 (a fairy-tale page; patterns moved onto
  today's clothes). Weakest: С18 manga (wooden bodies, simple faces). Wikimedia answered "too many requests" in this
  session; agents took samples elsewhere and listed links in `notes.md`.
- `to3d/r45…r52` (list: `to3d/items.json`): Р45 Марат ukiyo-e, Р46 Кира cut paper, Р47 Аня noir, Р48 Савелий pixel
  art, Р49 Вера Bilibin, Р50 Марат low-poly, Р51 Кира Soviet, Р52 Вера manga — each by `docs/briefs/to3d.md`
  (second round included): fitted to the drawing, a live face, Kimodo motions through the engine, a viewer in the
  «3D» tab. All but Р46 came out good. Ways worth repeating: Р50 rigs the style's own facet model on SOMA; Р48 renders
  low and lets the pixel rules decide each pixel (the 3D only answers material, light and depth); Р45, Р51, Р52 draw
  every frame with the style's own line, print or screentone from the 3D (the Р12 way); Р47 keeps noir's black as
  designed shapes, the shadow edge fixed on drawn lines while the light moves.

**How, to repeat it.** One agent per folder with the brief named above; the orchestrator committed and pushed as
results came, looked at each, and opened all eight «3D» tabs headless (no page errors).

**Seen.** The same weak spots as before: realistic Anny hands against drawn ones, clothes and hair as shells, Kimodo's
idle stooping and its wave bringing a short arm to a big head; profiles where the drawing's front and side disagree.
