# 2026-10-31 — breadth: tools, Anny, heroes, heads

**What called for it.** The page of body styles gave no beautiful bodies. Asked what we do wrong instrumentally, the
answer was seven things (cage, landmarks, Anny's own space, adapting mesh, the right smoothing, face and hair builders,
clothes with their own form); Pavel asked to do them all. He wants breadth now — a wide spread of forms, to find the
principles of beauty and learn to make the characters he wants; motion, poses and scenes not in focus. Along the way he
sent references (cartoon-yet-anatomical faces, indie rosters, expressive faces, anime and cel heroes, a doll-like 3D
girl, K-pop Demon Hunters) and asked to make those kinds of characters and, specifically, the heads from his pictures.

**What was done** (each with an experiment and a note in [directions.md](../directions.md)):
- Even smoothing in centimetres for simplification — first by hand on the form page (uniform Laplacian scaled by local
  edge length, girth restored along the normal), then as a cotangent solve with the other tools.
- Two directions: the designed form primary (Anny pulled onto drawn contours) and judging on a dressed person with a
  face; then both joined.
- Deformation tools: cage, face by points, subdivision (harmless, little gain; remeshing rejected — it would break
  garment binding and the engine's fixed body mesh).
- Anny's own shape space, run from her sources: proportions made by people; a head of another age on an adult body.
- Expressive faces and rubber-hose limbs: 11 toon characters, about 5 appealing.
- Indie heroes from simple forms on SOMA joints: 18, then 24 with colour and outline, faces with feelings, poses with a
  line of action; about 17 charming. Pavel: "о классссс! :3", "развей их, делай ещё и стильнее". Ten Kimodo motions
  ordered in the studio (walk, wave, jump, sit hugging knees, run, idle, dance, bow, sneak, angry stomp) to put on them.
- Dressed characters with faces: 72 generated, about 12 pleasant. Pavel: bodies and clothes look good; faces almost
  everywhere poor — eyes all the same and not real, plasticine hair, no ears, noses not right.
- Heads from anime and drawn references: 9 heads with drawn faces. Pavel: a good start, but proportions of face, chin,
  cheekbones, noses and mouths are not conveyed; the faces came out alike, only re-dressed and re-coloured, while the
  characters have clearly different faces — work each feature from its reference. Sent back to be redone.
- Lists of open things that already exist ([open-source.md](../open-source.md)) and what it takes to make each kind of
  character ([references.md](../references.md)).

**How, to repeat it.** Each experiment folder has a README; pictures are looked at in headless Chromium and tiled into
sheets; agents work in parallel, each in its own copy of a page, and report sheets that are looked at before showing.

each picture's angle. Pavel also asked for this file's rule to be written down: [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) — history
always, per commit or group of commits.

  neck cut off under the chin where the designed head meets Anny's body.

- A 3D view in the gallery (Pavel: "чтобы я мог вращать каждую модель, заставить её анимировать движениями кимодо"):
  each path with a 3D final has a "3D" tab that opens its page in viewer mode (`?viewer`) on the same page — pick the

  the legs in walking and sitting; hair (В1) only turns. Paths without a 3D view: Т2–Т5, Г2, Г4, И1.
- Clothes moved onto the good bodies (direction A and cage silhouettes, moderate stylisation); layers fixed (lower
  garments first, the top laid over them). Cloth physics: the skirt with sweater in the jump fixed; sitting and
  MakeHuman's front slit not solved.
- Deformations by lines and shapes made strong (Pavel: «покажи, чтобы работало! и трапеции, всякое»): line of action,
  squash and stretch, limb arcs, contrapposto, body as one shape — on the real Anny body with the skeleton moving.
- 3D-cartoon heads on a new tool: each face drawn as a solid read off its picture, then Anny's head mesh laid onto it
  with lids and lips cut along mesh edges, and an overlay check against the reference. 7 of 14 heads built, none
  exact (egg skulls, eyes as holes, mouths as grooves).
- Wrapped heads (Г3) finished for now: shaped open mouths drawn over the jaw-moved mesh, smiles with lifting corners
  and smiling eyes, the old man's ball nose and the semi-real man's long nose as their own pieces, no cast shadows on
  faces, shading from the designed surface's normals.
- Clothes of fabric (`experiments/cloth`, pictures in `docs/pictures/cloth`). Pavel asked for clothes that are cloth,
  not a mask on the body: they don't go through the body, crumple, bend, take the wind, and behave logically when a
  person walks, runs, lies down, tumbles and rolls; ordinary clothes of today (jacket, cardigan or sweater, trousers,
  skirt, shorts, swimsuit, blouse, scarf, dress, T-shirt) in fabrics of different kinds, on a woman, a man and a child.
  Done as XPBD cloth with small steps (16 a frame): fabric mass, stretch that gives way to pushing (so it folds),
  dihedral bending with the fabric's own rigidity, air on each triangle, collisions with the body and the garments under
  with friction, holds at waistband, neckline and cuffs with tethers, and sixteen fabrics (chiffon to tweed, lycra,
  neoprene). Garments: MakeHuman's subdivided; skirt, blouse, cardigan, jacket and swimsuit made on the body here; the
  scarf a flat strip. New Kimodo motions ordered in the studio: kneel and lie down, forward roll, and (going on from
  lying) rolling over. What took the most: bending that blew up when applied every other step; a skirt crumpling until
  lengths were solved after bending too; sleeves and trousers coming off limbs where body parts touch (hands into hips,
  legs pressed together, knee pits) — fixed by keeping arms a little off the body, a mitten hand, sleeves and legs kept
  on their own limb, pinched points left in place, outer garments meeting inner ones only where they lie together.
  Sitting on the floor (unsolved before: the skirt slid under the knees) now drapes over the knees. Left: in a deep knee
  bend the cloth can poke through at the knee for a few frames; clothes that ride up (jeans when kneeling, a T-shirt in
  a roll) stay up; computing is 0.1–0.4 s a frame, so the page computes a motion first and then plays it.
