# 2026-11-12 — four more styles (С17–С20) and one 3D character from each new style (Р45–Р50)

**What called for it.** Pavel asked for three or four more styles unlike the sixteen; I proposed Bilibin and Art
Nouveau, black-and-white seinen manga, low-poly facets and Soviet animation of the 1960s–70s (spares: Silver-Age
American comics, felt and knit toys), and he asked to run four Opus agents on them. Then he asked to turn one or two
random characters of the new styles into 3D the way Р1–Р12 were made, with Fable agents.

**What was done.**
- `styles/s17-bilibin`, `s18-manga`, `s19-lowpoly`, `s20-soviet` (list: `styles/items.json`): each by part B of
  `docs/briefs/styles-more.md` — samples, rules, all six characters (the four and Марат, Кира). Best: С20 (Khitruk's
  people, Марат and Кира), С19 (a line of faceted figurines, Марат), С17 (a fairy-tale page; patterns moved onto
  today's clothes). Weakest: С18 manga (wooden bodies, simple faces). Wikimedia answered "too many requests" in this
  session; agents took samples elsewhere and listed links in `notes.md`.
- `to3d/r45…r50` (list: `to3d/items.json`; numbers as they are in main — see the end of this note): Р45 Аня noir,
  Р46 Савелий pixel art, Р47 Вера Bilibin, Р48 Вера manga, Р49 Марат low-poly, Р50 Кира Soviet (and two more made
  but not brought in: Марат ukiyo-e, Кира cut paper) — each by `docs/briefs/to3d.md`
  (second round included): fitted to the drawing, a live face, Kimodo motions through the engine, a viewer in the
  «3D» tab. All six came out good. Ways worth repeating: Р49 rigs the style's own facet model on SOMA; Р46 renders
  low and lets the pixel rules decide each pixel (the 3D only answers material, light and depth); Р48, Р50 draw
  every frame with the style's own line, print or screentone from the 3D (the Р12 way); Р45 keeps noir's black as
  designed shapes, the shadow edge fixed on drawn lines while the light moves.

**How, to repeat it.** One agent per folder with the brief named above; the orchestrator committed and pushed as
results came, looked at each, and opened all eight «3D» tabs headless (no page errors).

**Seen.** The same weak spots as before: realistic Anny hands against drawn ones, clothes and hair as shells, Kimodo's
idle stooping and its wave bringing a short arm to a big head; profiles where the drawing's front and side disagree.

**Into main (same day).** Meanwhile another session brought its third round of drawings-to-3D into main as Р13–Р44
(`to3d/r13…r44`), and this branch's eight had been numbered `r13…r20`. Pavel decided: the third round keeps its
codes; these take the next free ones. Two of the eight repeated characters main already had in the same styles —
Марат in С13 (main's Р38) and Кира in С14 (main's Р40). Shown side by side, Марат came out about equal and Кира worse
(softer, papier-mâché), and Pavel decided not to bring these two in (they stay in this branch's history). The other
six became Р45–Р50, ordered by style: Р45 Аня noir (С15), Р46 Савелий pixel art (С16), Р47 Вера Bilibin (С17), Р48
Вера manga (С18), Р49 Марат low-poly (С19), Р50 Кира Soviet (С20). Renumbered everywhere the number is written:
folders (`git mv`), `to3d/items.json` (main's 44 lines, then these 6), each page's `entry.json` and `notes.md`, paths
in `tools/`, the `rNN/character` format tags in the data, this note and its name; the cache folders outside the
repository renamed to match. The styles keep С17–С20 (main ends at С16). `main` was merged into the branch first (the
only conflict, `to3d/items.json`, resolved as above), `docs/status.md` and `docs/findings.md` updated, and every page
opened from the folder above the repository, as on the site, before the push to `main`.
