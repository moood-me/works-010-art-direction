# What already exists

Open or free things that already do parts of this work — pages where you see the result at once, without installing.
Licences as found on 2026-10-31; check before use.

**Stylised characters to look at and turn**
- [VRoid Hub](https://hub.vroid.com/) — thousands of anime characters made in VRoid Studio (body, face, hair, clothes by
  sliders), each viewable in the browser, with a photo booth for poses and animation. The VRM format carries spring
  bones for hair and skirts, a set of face expressions, a toon shader.
- [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html) — simple stylised rigged and animated
  characters; long CC0, newer packs reportedly under the Quaternius Asset Licence (also free; unverified).
- [KayKit](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit) — chibi adventurers, skeletons and more, rigged and animated; the closest
  to indie heroes.
- [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets) — simple blocky characters, CC0.
- Charmorph (Blender add-on, stylised base bodies), UMA (Unity), Mixamo (free, not open; characters and auto-rig).

**From a picture to a 3D model**
- [Hunyuan3D-2](https://huggingface.co/spaces/tencent/Hunyuan3D-2) — official demo: an image in, a 3D model out.
- [CharacterGen](https://charactergen.github.io) — one drawing of a stylised character to a 3D model in A-pose (trained
  on VRoid Hub data).
- [TRELLIS](https://microsoft.github.io/TRELLIS) — Microsoft, MIT; gallery on the project page.

**A skeleton on any form**
- [Make-It-Animatable](https://huggingface.co/spaces/jasongzy/Make-It-Animatable) — demo: any model in, skeleton and
  motion out (Apache-2.0).
- [UniRig](https://zjp-shadow.github.io/works/UniRig/) — skeletons and skin weights for very different characters; its
  successor is SkinTokens.
- Rigify (Blender) — open auto-rig, with cartoon variants and bendy limbs.

**Face**
- [Audio2Face](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Audio2Face-3D-SDK) — the face speaking from the voice; NVIDIA open-sourced the
  models, SDK and training framework in September 2025.
- ARKit's 52 face shapes — the common standard for brows, lids, mouth.
- [Inochi2D](https://github.com/Inochi2D/inochi2d) — open (BSD) 2D puppet animation, a Live2D alternative; being
  rewritten.
