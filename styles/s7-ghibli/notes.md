# С7 — Гибли: рисованное тёплое

## What was done

1. **Samples.** Studio Ghibli publishes stills of its films for free use "within common sense"
   (https://www.ghibli.jp/info/013344/; files at `https://www.ghibli.jp/gallery/<film><NNN>.jpg`). Downloaded
   the first 50 stills of Totoro, Kiki, Spirited Away, Howl, The Wind Rises, Whisper of the Heart, Ponyo and Only
   Yesterday (400 frames, kept in `/home/user/cache/styles-s7-ghibli/dl/`), looked at them as contact sheets, then at
   the best ones full size. Kept ten in `refs/` (≤ 800 px, jpg), chosen for how ordinary people are drawn: a young
   woman's face close up, Sophie, a tired mother, an old man and a boy, children laughing, a mother and son, an old
   woman, Kiki, a figure in a painted field.
2. **Rules** — measured on the samples (e.g. on Sophie, `howl001`: eyes at ≈55% of the head from the crown, the
   bottom of the nose ≈75%, the mouth ≈86%), written into `entry.json` («Правила стиля»).
3. **Drawing.** Every picture is SVG generated by the Python scripts in `tools/` (hand-made forms, no tracing, no
   image models). The drawing is built from simple 3D forms projected to flat shapes, so the same character turns to
   any view (front, three-quarter, side) and keeps its face:
   - `geo.py` — orthographic view (yaw, pitch, scale), lofts and tubes → flat polygons (shapely), svg paths.
   - `svgdoc.py` — the page: fill, outline, the one shadow tone (`shade`: the colour multiplied by a warm lavender),
     painted-edge filter, colour mottling, grain, rendering with headless Chrome. `MODE` = line / flat / full
     (for the rules picture).
   - `head.py` — the head: horizontal rings (half width, front, back per height) + a bulge along the middle
     (`profile()`: bridge, nose, lips, chin) + an optional cheekbone. Features are 2D templates laid on the surface:
     eye (lids, iris, glints, lid shadow, lashes), brow (or a bushy old man's brow), nose mark (hook / round / dash),
     mouth (line, D, O, shout; teeth, tongue), lower lip, ears, blush, wrinkles (`marks`), `patch()` for any shape on
     the face. `Expr` holds what the face does (open, lids, brows, mouth, smile, look, blush).
   - `hair.py` — the hair: a shell over the skull between a hairline and an optional top line (bald crown), locks
     (tapered ribbons from a root on the shell), `tufts` (clumps that follow the shell down and break the dome),
     `balls` (a bun), `flow` strand lines. Front hair is one flat shape drawn over the face (it casts its shadow on the
     forehead); locks rooted on the far side go behind the head.
   - `body.py` — the skeleton from measures `B` and a pose (directions of upper arm, forearm, hand, thigh, shin;
     lean, hip shift, head turn), the torso loft, limbs, hands (palm, four fingers, thumb — each drawn with its own
     line), shoes.
   - `dress.py` — puts a character together far to near: legs and shoes, trousers as one piece, torso garments
     (top with stripes, buttons, collar, turtleneck, neck cut; an open cardigan/jacket/vest), sleeves joined into the
     shoulder, a held prop, arms, then the head; `head_drawer()` is the usual head function.
   - `backdrop.py`, `scenes.py` — painted places: sky with cumulus, foliage from leaf clumps in three tones, grass,
     flowers; the meadow, the workshop, the thicket, the study.
   - `marat.py`, `kira.py` — the two added in round three (see below).
   - `anya.py`, `savely.py`, `tim.py`, `vera.py` — the characters: `FACE`, `HAIRDEF`, `B`, `POSES`, `SPEC`, `extra()`
     (moustache and loupe, a plaster, earrings), props (`hat`, `watch`).
   - `build.py` — composes and renders all pictures.
4. **By eye.** Rendered and compared side by side with the stills many times. What changed on the way: the first
   faces had the features too high and a long chin (measured Sophie and moved nose and mouth down); eyes were anime
   almonds with an outline round the white (now the Ghibli eye: heavy lid, short lower stroke, no outline); the head
   was too deep, so in three-quarter view a huge cheek lay between eye and ear (flattened the face, ear forward);
   the shadow was grey (now a warm multiply); fringes were cut straight (now clumps with gaps); hair was a smooth
   helmet (tufts); necks were long tubes with a round bottom (now the neckline cuts the neck); shoulders had pads
   (sleeve tops trimmed to the shoulder slope); Anya's tail first crossed her face (now it comes from behind the neck
   over the near shoulder); Vera's bob turned into "mouse ears" and was replaced by a low knot.

## How to repeat

```
cd styles/s7-ghibli
python3 tools/build.py            # all pictures → svg/ and pics/*.jpg (headless google-chrome, about a minute)
python3 tools/build.py portrait   # only names that contain "portrait"
```

## How to add a new character

1. Copy one of `anya.py` / `vera.py` (a woman), `savely.py` (an old man), `tim.py` (a child) to `tools/<name>.py`.
2. `FACE`: the rings `a` (half width per height), `zf`/`zb` (front/back), `prof=profile(nose_y, nose, mouth_y=…)`;
   then eye (`eye_x/y/w/h`, `iris_w/h`, `lid_w`, `lashes`, `crease`), `brow_*`, `nose` style, `mouth_*`, `ear`,
   `cheek`, `blush`, `marks` (wrinkles as head-local points; mirrored unless `both=False`). Head units: the head is 2
   tall, crown +1, chin −1; keep the Ghibli layout — eyes ≈ −0.09, nose ≈ −0.50, mouth ≈ −0.72.
3. `HAIRDEF`: `hairline` (azimuth° → height; 0 = front, 90 = the character's left), `volume`, optional `top`
   (bald), `locks`, `tufts`, `balls`, `flow`.
4. `B` (centimetres; torso rings, joints, radii, `head_c`, `head_h`), `POSES` (at least `stand`), `SPEC` (skin,
   top/over/bottom/shoes, optional `prop`).
5. Register it in `CAST` in `build.py` (module, portrait pose, figure pose, place) and give it a calm `Expr` in
   `CALM` (or keep `CALM` and its own `EXPR` in the module, as `marat.py` and `kira.py` do). Look at it with `python3 tools/build.py <name>` and against the stills.

## Round three: Марат and Кира

Asked for by `docs/briefs/styles-more.md` (part A): two more characters, unlike the four, drawn by this style's rules
with its code; the four's pictures stay as they were.

What was drawn: for each of the two a portrait, a full figure, a turnaround and an expression sheet
(`portrait-`, `figure-`, `turn-`, `expr-` + `marat` / `kira`), and all six together (`six`). The four's files were not
re-rendered (their `pics/` and `svg/` are byte for byte the old ones).

How, so it can be repeated:

- `tools/marat.py`. A broad head on a short neck, a body half as wide again as anyone's (`B`), `figure_scale=4.7` in
  `SPEC` so the giant fits the same sheet. Curls are `balls` — round clumps along the edge of a low hair shell. The
  beard is a second `Hair` on the same head (`BEARD`): its "hairline" is the chin, its `top` is the beard's upper
  edge by azimuth (under the nose, across the cheek, up to the sideburn), `balls` curl its lower edge; `extra()`
  draws it over the face and then draws the mouth again on top (an open laugh with teeth and tongue; a shut mouth in
  a black beard gets a lighter line). His resting face is a laugh (`CALM` in the module). The apron (`SPEC['apron']`)
  and the towel (`SPEC['worn']`) are cloth patches on the torso's form; the ladle is the `prop` of the `ladle` pose.
- `tools/kira.py`. A narrower face with a small chin, eyes smaller than Аня's with a heavy lid line, low straight
  brows; short choppy hair, three long fringe locks from a parting on her right down over her left eye. The slouch is
  in the poses: `lean`, `head_fwd`, `shrug`, the head nodded down, the look coming back from under the brows.
  `worn()` draws the hood's roll round the neck, the headphones on it, the boots over the narrow trousers (acid
  laces), and closes the pocket over the hand that is inside. Her own `EXPR`: joy is a crooked half-smile and a
  blush, anger is bared teeth.
- Shared code, extended (nothing the four use changed; checked by regenerating their SVG — identical before and after):
  `Expr.smirk` (one corner of the mouth up), hand kind `pocket` (a hidden hand), sleeve `roll` (a rolled sleeve),
  trousers `narrow`, `SPEC['worn']` (things over body and arms, under the head), `SPEC['figure_scale']`,
  `dress.surface_patch` (cloth lying on a form), `F.expr` (the expression, for `extra`), a module's own `CALM` and
  `EXPR`, `turnaround(name, calm=True)`, the places `kitchen` and `dusk` in `scenes.py`, `cast6()` in `build.py`.
- By eye, what changed on the way: Марат's first eyes were slits and the mouth small (bigger eye, wider laugh, head
  thrown back); the beard's cheek line went too high and made the face narrow (lowered); the curls were an afro
  (smaller clumps, a scalloped forehead line); the cook's stand collar looked like a bib (removed); the towel moved
  out onto the shoulder. Кира's headphones were twice too big; trousers flared (now `narrow`) and hid the boots.
- `head_rot`'s second number: positive tilts the face **up**, negative down (the comment in `head.py` says the
  opposite).
- `python3 tools/build.py marat`, `… kira`, `… six` build only the new pictures. A full `python3 tools/build.py`
  re-renders the four as well: their SVG comes out identical except `cast.svg`, where Тим's fringe differs a little
  from the committed file (so it was before this round) — don't run the full build unless that is wanted.

Weak, honestly: Марат's rolled sleeves are balls and his shoulders have straight corners (the sleeve tops are cut
by a box — fine for hanging arms, visible on his); the hand on his hip is a paddle; in profile the beard is a thin
strip. Кира's face under the fringe lives on one eye; in three-quarter views her head reads as tilted further than
the pose says (the jaw line and the fringe run parallel); the hood is a roll, not a hood. Legs are still smooth
tubes for both.

## Honest state

The language of the style reads — face layout, eye, nose and mouth marks, one line, one warm shadow tone, painted
backdrops — but the drawing is computed from forms, not led by a hand: poses are stiff, limbs are smooth tubes,
clothes have few folds, hands are simple, hair has no flowing locks, the profile is weaker than the front and
three-quarter views. Backdrops are a sketch of Ghibli's gouache, not painting.
