## What was done

1. **Samples.** Arcane (Fortiche, 2021–2024) — stills, model sheets, head turnarounds, art-book pages — taken from the
   Arcane wiki (arcane.fandom.com, its file API), looked at, and the 10 best kept as small copies in `refs/` (third-party,
   kept only as references), plus Pavel's `illustrated-semireal.png` from `docs/pictures/references/`.
2. **Rules** written from what was seen in them — on the page (`entry.json`, block «Правила стиля»).
3. **The cast drawn by the rules** — Аня, Савелий Петрович, Тим, Вера: portraits, full figures, head-and-shoulder
   turnarounds for all four (front, three-quarter, side), full-figure turnarounds for Аня and Савелий, expression sheets
   for Аня and Тим, the four together, and a sheet of how a head is built.

## How the pictures are made (all SVG, generated by our own scripts)

The style is near-real 3D form with painted, graphic light — so the drawing is done the same way, in vector:

- `tools/arc.py` — a tiny vector "painter". A head, a neck, a torso, a skirt are **lofts**: stacked super-elliptic
  cross-sections (width, front and back depth, exponent per height) plus gaussian bumps (brow ridge, sockets,
  cheekbones, lips, chin, jowls). Arms, legs and hands are **tubes** swept along a 3D curve. For a view (yaw) each
  surface is sampled on a parameter grid; fields `n·view` and `n·light` are cut by marching squares (`skimage`) into
  regions — silhouette, shadow, deep shadow, highlight, rim, half-tone — which are mapped back onto the surface,
  projected and written as smooth SVG paths. This gives the big graphic light-and-shadow shapes of Arcane and keeps
  every view (front, ¾, side) consistent.
- `tools/face.py`, `tools/person.py` — features drawn in the face's own flat coordinates (eye almond with heavy upper
  lid, iris with a lit lower half, lash line with a wing, crease, brows as blocks with hairs, lips, nostrils, wrinkles,
  moustache, glasses) and **wrapped onto the head surface**, so they turn with it. Expressions are a few numbers (brow
  inner/outer, knit, lid, lower lid, squint, smile per side, open, teeth, snarl, gaze). Hair = an offset shell of the
  skull masked by a hairline + many tapered locks generated between guide locks (each with a gradient by light, inner
  strand lines, a dark separation edge); locks that pass behind the head are cut at the silhouette.
- Painterly finish by SVG filters: `brush` (displaced edge + streaky alpha) on shadow shapes, `paint` (slight
  displacement) on fills, soft blurs for blush and half-tones, form-following brush strokes on the skin, a canvas
  texture on the backdrop and a fine grain over all.
- `tools/cast.py` — the four people: head tables, features, palettes, hair, clothes and poses. `tools/body.py` —
  figure parts (tubes, shoes, hands, a box for a book or a folder) drawn back to front.
- `tools/make.py` — builds every picture into `svg/` and renders it with headless Chrome into `pics/`.

Repeat: `pip install numpy shapely scikit-image`, then `cd tools && python3 make.py` (or `make.py portraits turns expr
figures cast construction`, or one item like `portrait-anya`). PNGs are then converted to JPEG for the page
(`convert x.png -quality 86 x.jpg`).
