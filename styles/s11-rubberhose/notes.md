# С11 — Резиновые 1930-е

What was done (Claude, one agent of the "Стили персонажей" run; the brief is `/home/user/cache/BRIEF-styles.md`): found
samples of 1930s rubber-hose animation (Fleischer, early Disney, Ub Iwerks) and of Cuphead, its modern heir; looked at
Pavel's favourite П2 (`docs/pictures/toon/`) for the hose limbs and faces; wrote the style's rules from the samples;
drew the cast — Аня, Савелий Петрович, Тим, Вера — in SVG by those rules: four portraits (round medallions), four
posed full figures, turnarounds (front, three-quarter, profile) for all four, expression sheets for all four (neutral,
joy, surprise, sadness, anger), the four together in colour and on black-and-white film, two rule sheets.

## How it was drawn

All SVG is generated by hand-written Python in `tools/`; no tracing, no image models.

- `tools/lib.py` — the shared parts of the style:
  - `smooth(points)` — Catmull-Rom through points → cubic Bézier: every form is written as a few points round its
    outline; `blob`, `line`, `tline` (a tapered brush stroke), `ellipse`, `circle`, `g` (place/rotate/scale/mirror),
    `clip`, `union` (several shapes inked as one form: an ink pass under all, then the fill pass).
  - `hose(pts, w, fill, w1, w2)` — a rubber limb: one arc without elbow or knee, may taper.
    `arm(pts, w, fill, pose, scale, flip)` ends in a glove that follows the arm; `leg(...)` ends in a shoe.
  - `Head(R, a)` — the ball head turned by angle `a` (0 front, ~.55 three-quarter, 1.25 profile, facing the viewer's
    right). `project(x, y[, z])` puts a feature drawn on the front of the ball where it lands in the turn and says how
    much it foreshortens; `ball()`, `muzzle(...)` (the lower face as a second, flatter ball that bulges forward in
    profile), `cap(face_loop, colour)` (hair = the ball minus the bare face, so a hairline drawn once works in every
    turn), `loop(points)`.
  - `eye(...)` (kinds `pie` with the cut wedge, `ring`, `dot`, `spark`, `closed-happy`, `closed`; lid with tilt, heavy
    upper lid, lashes), `brow`, `mouth` (smile, grin, o, shout, frown, wobble, flat, women's lips), `nose` (button,
    bulb, pointed), `cheek`, `glove(pose)` (open, wave, fist, point, rest, hold), `shoe(view)`, `shadow`.
  - the film look in `svg(...)`: paper background, ink wobble (`feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap`, scale 2.4), grain,
    vignette; `bw=True` turns the whole sheet into black-and-white film.
- `tools/anya.py`, `savely.py`, `tim.py`, `vera.py` — one module per character, each with the same face:
  `R` (head radius), `FEET` (y of the shoe soles, head centre at 0,0), `EXPR` (expressions as parameters),
  `head(a, expr)`, `figure(view, pose, expr)` with views `front`, `q`, `side` and poses `stand` plus one character
  pose (`dream`, `watch`, `sneak`, `books`).
- `tools/build.py` — the sheets (`portrait`, `figure`, `turnaround`, `expressions` with squash and stretch, `cast`,
  `rules`); `CAST` lists the characters with their pose and portrait colour.
- `tools/render.js` (Playwright, headless Chrome) → PNG; `tools/tojpg.py` → JPEG (the grain makes PNG ~1–2 MB each).

## Repeat

```
cd styles/s11-rubberhose/tools
python3 build.py                                                               # writes ../svg/*.svg
NODE_PATH=/opt/node-tools/node_modules node render.js ../pics 1 ../svg/*.svg   # → ../pics/*.png
python3 tojpg.py ../pics                                                       # → ../pics/*.jpg
```

## Adding a new character

1. Copy the module closest to it (a girl → `anya.py`, an old man → `savely.py`, a child → `tim.py`, a tall adult →
   `vera.py`) to `tools/<name>.py`; change the palette constants at the top, `R`, `EXPR`.
2. In `head()`: keep the order — what is behind the head (back hair, bun, ears), `union([H.ball(), H.muzzle(...)])` for
   the face, cheeks, eyes (`eye` at `H.project(±x, y)`), brows, nose, mouth, then hair with `H.cap(face_loop)` and what
   sits on top. Place features by (x, y) on the front of the ball: they turn by themselves.
3. In `figure()`: legs first, then body (clothes are `smooth` point lists; shift front details by `s = sin(a)` for the
   three-quarter view), arms with `arm()`, head last. The profile is its own function (`figure_side`) — bodies there
   are drawn anew, narrower.
4. Measure where the soles land (render the figure over a line, see the history of `FEET` below) and set `FEET`.
5. Add it to `CAST` in `build.py`; build, render, look next to `refs/` and the other three; redraw what is weak.

Rules the drawings keep (so a new one fits): eyes nearly touching, pie-cut or ring pupils; limbs as one arc; white
gloves with a cuff; big round shoes with a shine; flat colour, one shine on shiny things; outline `LW` 3.2 at R≈65,
inner lines 2.2; palette muted saturated on cream paper.

## How it went (iterations)

- First head: a moon face with small features and a helmet of hair — eyes made bigger and closer, hair rebuilt as
  "ball minus face" (`cap`), the lower face as a second ball (`muzzle`) so the profile has cheeks and a mouth that
  stand forward instead of a spike.
- Gloves: the first were boxy; rebuilt from round-ended sausages inked as one form.
- Савелий: the moustache first read as fangs (pointed scallops) — redrawn as one soft mass with three round lobes;
  brows enlarged into clouds hanging over the eyes, their tilt sign fixed (grumpy = inner ends down).
- Тим: the first mischief pose (leaning, slingshot) broke the legs — replaced by feet apart, slingshot behind the
  back, a finger at the chin.
- Вера: side waves added (the 1930s finger waves), smaller pupils (the large ones made her bug-eyed), a longer face
  and a line under the eyes for tiredness.
- Feet: `FEET` was the ankle, so everyone floated; measured the soles and set `FEET` to them.
- Black-and-white: the grey filter made skin mid-grey; the curve was lifted so skin goes near white, as on the film.

## Honest state

See `verdict` in `entry.json`. In short: the style reads (closest to Cuphead in colour; the black-and-white sheet to
Fleischer 1930–35); Тим and Савелий are the liveliest; Аня is sweet but doll-like; Вера is the weakest (stiff pose,
plain face, hair like a cap). Poses are calmer than the samples; three-quarter bodies are only shifted and narrowed
front views; the line is even (the wobble is a filter, not a brush); and the style lives in bouncy motion, which a
still sheet does not have.

## Round three: Марат and Кира

What was done (Claude, one agent of round three; the brief is `docs/briefs/styles-more.md`, part A): two characters
added to the cast by this style's rules and code — a portrait, a posed figure, a turnaround and an expression sheet
for each, and all six together (`cast6`). The four were not touched: their SVG files are byte-for-byte the same after
the build (checked with `cmp` against a copy), so their pictures were not re-rendered.

- `tools/marat.py` — Марат, the cook. The biggest: `FEET` 516, a barrel torso about 220 wide on short thick legs, no
  neck. The head's silhouette is the ball plus the *volume of the beard* (a wider, deeper lower ball with curl bumps
  along its edge) inked as one form with `union`; the beard is then painted inside that silhouette through a clip, its
  upper edge (sideburns → under the cheeks → under the nose) drawn on the ball with `H.loop`, so it turns with the
  head. Short curls: a ring of small circles behind `H.cap`, and the same circles without ink over it, so the cap's
  own outline does not show inside the hair; the ring's front end climbs to the hairline as the head turns. His own
  mouth `grin()` — an open D with a row of upper teeth, a tongue and a lower lip — because a dark mouth in a dark beard
  reads as a hole; the other mouths get a lip under them for the same reason. Resting face = the grin with the lower
  lids pushed up (`lower`). Poses: `stand`, `laugh` (the figure: torso and head leaned back as one group, fist on the
  hip, ladle up), `hello` (the portrait: the ladle by the shoulder, so the hand stays inside the medallion).
- `tools/kira.py` — Кира, the teenager. A narrower, longer lower face, smaller eyes with a dark upper lid, low
  straight brows, a pressed crooked mouth (`pressed`, `smirk`, `teeth` are drawn in the module); a choppy cap, pointed
  locks (`tuft`) sticking out round the lower edge of the hair, a long three-bladed fringe over the far eye drawn on
  the ball with `H.pts` (in profile its blades are shortened to a bang, or they would cover the near eye). The slouch
  is `sway(y, k)` — a sideways shift down the body (shoulders back, hips forward) applied to every point of the
  hoodie, arms and hips; the head is put forward and down and rotated. In the figure the head is scaled by `HS` .92
  (a teenager's head is smaller against the body than a child's). Poses: `stand`, `sulk`.
- `tools/lib.py` — `arm()` got optional `w1`, `w2` (the hose's width in the middle and at the wrist: a thick forearm)
  and `sleeve=(fill, t)` (a rolled-up sleeve over the first part of the arm, ending in a cuff). Without them it draws
  as before.
- `tools/build.py` — `CAST` has the two with optional keys (`ppose`, `fexpr`, `top`, `tsc`, `esc`, `ey`, `fx`);
  `cast6()` is the sheet of six; the new sheets are built after the four's (ids in the SVG are counted through the
  whole build, so this order keeps the four's files unchanged).

Repeat (only the new sheets are rendered; `tojpg.py` converts whatever PNG is in `pics/`):

```
cd styles/s11-rubberhose/tools
python3 build.py
NODE_PATH=/opt/node-tools/node_modules node render.js ../pics 1 ../svg/cast6.svg ../svg/*-marat.svg ../svg/*-kira.svg
python3 tojpg.py ../pics
```

How it went: Марат's first head had the brows under the hair (hairline raised, brows drawn last) and an afro (curls
made smaller); the body was a rectangle with thin arms (chest widened to a barrel, forearms thickened, the profile
deepened). Кира's first fringe left the far eye open (blades moved over it), two locks on the crown read as a bow
(removed), the hand at the headphones read as a stub on the chest (replaced by a fist hanging from a long sleeve).

Марат's profile (the back of the head is one dark mass) and his hair, fuller than "short curls"; Кира's hands (one
in the pocket, the other half behind the hip) and her straight, stiff turnaround. Scratch renders are in
`/home/user/cache/styles-s11-rubberhose/scratch/`.
