# С1 — Pixar and Disney cartoon: notes

## What was done

1. **Samples.** I downloaded concept art, model sheets and stills of Luca, Encanto, Tangled, Big Hero 6,
   Turning Red and Coco (sources below) and looked through them. I kept 12 as small jpgs (≤ 800 px) in `refs/`.
   Downloads and contact sheets are in `/home/user/cache/styles-s1-pixar/` (`dl/`, `f1/`–`f3/`, `sheet*.jpg`),
   outside the repository.
2. **Rules.** I wrote them from what the samples show: Mirabel's and Ming's face models, the Luca and Rapunzel
   expression sheets, the Encanto models. They are in `entry.json`, block «Правила стиля».
3. **Drawing.** The drawings are SVG generated by my own Python scripts in `tools/`. Nothing is traced and no
   image model is used. The approach:
   - `pix.py`: a head is a small 3D model. It is a stack of superellipse cross-sections (`w`, `zf`, `zb` keys over
     the height) with Gaussian bumps for the brow, nose, lips, chin, cheeks and sockets. It is projected with yaw,
     pitch and roll, and its silhouette is taken per height. Volume is painted the way a concept artist paints it,
     by `paint_volume`: a shadow-coloured base, then the same shape in the base colour, shifted toward the light
     (upper left) and blurred inside its clip, plus an optional rim.
   - `face.py`: the features are written in front-view coordinates and wrapped onto the head surface, so one face
     turns into three-quarter and profile views:
     - eye: lid band, lash line, iris, catchlights;
     - brows, nose made of shadow and light, mouth with an opening, teeth and tongue, ears;
     - hair: ribbons (locks) on an ellipsoid shell, sorted into a back pass and a front pass, with strands and a
       sheen; a smooth cap for sleek hair, cut by the hairline and the silhouette meridians; a bun.
   - `body.py` / `person.py`: torso and garments are sweeps (the same sections). Limbs are tapered tubes through
     joints, the hands are a palm plus tubes for the fingers, the shoes are hulls. All parts are drawn in depth
     order for any yaw.
   - `cast.py`: the four characters as data (head shape, colours, hair, clothes, joints). `build.py`: the
     pictures (poses, expressions, compositions).
4. **Iterating by eye.** Each picture was rendered and looked at next to the samples. What was redone:
   - the eyes went from anime-like outlined almonds to a lid of skin and a lash line with no lower contour;
   - the brows were raised and thickened;
   - the nose went from dots to shadow and light;
   - Anya's hair went from thin "dreadlocks" to wide sweeps over a back mass;
   - Vera's hair went from spiky ribbons to a smooth cap with a swoop and a bun; Savely's tufts were redone;
   - the jaws of the women were softened;
   - the necks were shortened and moved back;
   - hands about 2x larger;
   - expressions pushed further;
   - a bug where the back of every section was mirrored to the front was fixed (`zb` sign).

## How to repeat

```
cd styles/s1-pixar
python3 tools/build.py            # every picture; or: python3 tools/build.py portrait
```

Needs `google-chrome` (headless screenshot of the SVG) and ImageMagick `convert` (png -> webp). The full-size PNGs
go to `/home/user/cache/styles-s1-pixar/render/`. `svg/` holds the drawings and `pics/` the webp copies shown on the
page.

## Honest state

The faces work best: Аня, Тим and Савелий Петрович read as pleasant cartoon people. Вера is the weakest face.

The weak parts:
- the bodies are wooden: tube arms, small, unclear hands, no gesture or line of action;
- clothes have no folds and no weight;
- Anya's hair ends look like noodles;
- Vera's hair reads as a helmet.

Because every view comes from one simple model, the turnarounds are consistent, but the shapes come out generic.

## Round three: Марат and Кира

What was asked: two more characters in the same style, beside the four (brief `docs/briefs/styles-more.md`, part A).

What was done:
- `tools/marat.py`, `tools/kira.py` — a module per character (head, hair, body, clothes, props), like the four in
  `cast.py`. `tools/cloth.py` — shared marks on a garment (patch, seam, band, dot) and a strip of cloth (ribbon);
  unlike the decorations in `cast.py` they follow a bent figure.
- Shared code was extended, each time so that the four's pictures stay byte for byte the same
  (`python3 tools/check_four.py` draws them again and compares with `svg/`):
  - `person.py`: a pose (or a character, `P["bend"]`) may carry a **line of action** — `bend=dict(x=[(y, dx)…],
    z=[(y, dz)…])` shifts the whole figure (clothes, neck, head, joints) sideways and forward by splines over the
    height. Марат's laugh leans back on it; Кира's slouch is her habitual bend. Also: the `before_torso` hook
    (things behind the neck: the headphones' band, the towel's back half), `curl` by side, `palm` and `spread`
    of a hand by side.
  - `body.py`: `hand(..., palm=)` — where the palm looks, for a gesture (Марат's open hand).
  - `face.py`: a hair lock may be a **ball** (`ball=spec, r, turn`) — a round curl that looks the same from every
    side; `scalp_curls`, `jaw_curls` cover a scalp and a jaw with them (Марат's hair and beard). `teeth_h` in a
    head's data (taller teeth for a big laugh). A catchlight is drawn only while it is inside the eye opening
    (a laughing eye narrowed to a slit).
  - `build.py`: the two in `PEOPLE`, `PORTRAIT`, `FIGURE`, `FLOOR`; `CALM` (the face of a turnaround), `EXPR_OF`
    (a character's own expression sheet: Кира's joy is a smirk), `together6` → `cast6`.
- Iterating by eye, what was redone: Марат's curls went from flat comma-shaped ribbons (scales, shards at the
  silhouette) to balls, fewer and bigger, without the strand lines (they looked like coffee beans); his moustache
  now follows the mouth's line so the teeth show; his body was widened twice and deepened; the laughing eyes were
  opened a little so they aren't sleepy. Кира's eyes were made smaller and darker and the lids heavier (the first
  face was an anime doll), the hair went from purple to charcoal, the fringe was lengthened over the eye, the
  headphones moved from the chest up to the neck, the head's nod was turned the right way (in this code a
  **negative** `head_pitch` looks down).

Honest state: see the verdict. Weakest: Марат in profile; the hem corners of sweeps end in small spikes (the same
artefact as Аня's skirt); hands are still tubes.

## How to add a character

1. Copy `tools/kira.py` (a head from `woman_head`) or `tools/marat.py` (a head written whole) to `tools/<name>.py`.
   A head is data: sections `w`, `zf`, `zb`, `bumps`, the sizes of eye, brow, nose, mouth, colours, `hair`
   (a `Hair` of locks, balls or a cap). A person is data too: joints, radii of limbs, `layers` (sweeps of clothes
   with a `deco`), hooks for props, an optional `bend`.
2. In `build.py`: add the person to `PEOPLE`, a backdrop to `BG`, the floor height to `FLOOR`, a portrait and a
   figure pose to `PORTRAIT` and `FIGURE`; if needed `CALM`, `EXPR_OF`, `EXPR_S`; add jobs to `JOBS`.
3. `python3 tools/build.py <name>`; look at `/home/user/cache/styles-s1-pixar/render/*.png` next to the others.
4. If shared code was touched: `python3 tools/check_four.py` (it also guards any earlier character when its names
   are taken out of `NEW`).
