# С8 — Кукольная анимация (stop-motion)

## What was done

1. **Samples.** Behind-the-scenes and press photos of stop-motion puppets: Laika (Kubo — a Collider set-visit gallery
   with full-size photos of the puppets and replacement faces; Coraline turntable frames and ParaNorman/Coraline
   stills from laika.com), Guillermo del Toro's Pinocchio (Frame.io article), Aardman's Early Man (VFX Voice),
   Wendell & Wild (Nerdist), Isle of Dogs (Film Independent). Looked at all of them, kept twelve in `refs/`
   (≤ 800 px, jpg). `05` is three frames of Laika's Coraline turntable put side by side (front, ¾, side).
2. **Rules** — in `entry.json` (block «Правила стиля»), each from the samples.
3. **Drawing.** Every picture is SVG generated by the scripts in `tools/` (no tracing, no image models). The idea:
   a puppet is a small real object, so each head is a real 3D shape and every part is lit as a bump of material.
   - `sculpt.py` — the material kit: `Doc`; `form` (big shapes: the shape moved toward the key light and blurred
     gives the shadow side, a warm band at the terminator, edge occlusion, sheen, rim); `relief` (small parts:
     the blurred alpha as a height map lit by `feDiffuseLighting`/`feSpecularLighting`; negative height = a groove);
     `tex` (noise textures: clay, felt, cloth, knit); `studio` (dark set, warm glow, bokeh, floor), `puppet`
     (cool rim + warm edge over a whole puppet), `keylight` (fall-off), `finish` (vignette, grain), `render`.
   - `head.py` — `Head`: a loft of elliptic slices (tables `a` = half width, `front`/`back` = profile, optional
     `bumps` for cheekbones/chin/brow ridge, `x` = side offset) turned by yaw/roll; `frame()` lays flat features on
     the surface. Features: `eye` (glass ball in a socket, lids, painted iris, glint, lash line, lid paint, bags),
     `brow` (thin or bushy), `nose` (its own small loft, bridge fading into the face), `mouth`, `ear`, `wrinkle`,
     `blush`, `freckles`.
   - `hair.py` — sculpted hair: `Lock` (a ribbon along a 3D path in head units), `cap_polys` (the cap over the
     skull above a hairline, as strips, split into the half turned away and the half facing us), `mass` (union of
     locks + cap, shaded as one form; each lock cut with grooves and fine strand lines; a sheen band).
   - `puppet.py` — `draw_head(d, P, ex, S, cx, cy, yaw, roll)`: hair behind, far ear, head, features, hair in front;
     hooks `under_nose` (moustache) and `over_all` (glasses). `P['stage']` = 'bare' | 'face' for the construction
     picture.
   - `props.py` — glasses (round, half-moon, rectangular), moustache, loupe, bun.
   - `body.py` — `bust()`: neck, torso loft (clothes painted inside it before shading: stripes, panels), knit ribs,
     an open neckline with collarbones, a stand collar, details on the surface (`tx`, `front_patch`).
   - `figure.py` — `figure()`: the whole puppet; limbs as tubes along 3D paths, sculpted hands (relaxed / fist /
     open / hold), shoes, skirts as lofts, props; parts sorted by depth.
   - `cast.py` — the four characters: `ANYA`, `SAVELY`, `TIM`, `VERA` (head + face), `*_BODY` (bust), `*_FIG`
     (full figure), shared `EXPR` (calm, dream, joy, surprise, sad, anger, grin, stern, grumpy, tired).
   - `build.py` — composes and renders all pictures.
4. **By eye.** Rendered and looked many times. What changed on the way: the first heads were bald eggs with
   features like stickers (coordinates were rounded to 0.01 head — jagged lids; fixed the precision); hair as many
   thin tubes looked like dreadlocks, then like plastic — rebuilt as a few masses cut with grooves and strands, with
   narrow roots so they don't stick out of the crown; the hair cap was first a hand-built polygon and broke in ¾ —
   rebuilt from strips of the shell; the nose was a stick — its bridge now fades into the face; gaze now looks at
   the camera unless told otherwise; skins were too pink — desaturated; Savely's head was a box — made a pear with
   a big nose, bushy brows, half-moon glasses; Vera's slicked hair was messy — combed bands; arms in busts looked
   like pillars — busts have no separate arms. Savely's turnaround was dropped: in profile his flat moustache and
   tufts don't hold.

## How to repeat

```
cd styles/s8-stopmotion
python3 tools/build.py            # all pictures → svg/*.svgz and pics/*.jpg (headless google-chrome, ~1 min)
python3 tools/build.py portrait   # only names that contain "portrait"
```
Needs `google-chrome` and Python with Pillow. The SVGs are gzipped (`.svgz`, opens in a browser as is).

## How to add a new character

1. In `cast.py` copy one character dict (e.g. `ANYA`): change the head `shape` tables (`a` widths, `front`/`back`
   profile, `bumps`), the feature numbers (`eye_*`, `brow_*`, `nose_*`, `mouth_*`, `ear_*`), colours, `wrinkles`.
   Work in head units: head height = 1, y from −0.5 (crown) to 0.5 (chin), eyes a little below 0.
2. Write its hair function returning `dict(col, locks=[H.Lock(...)], cap=(hairline(phi), y_back, k))`; build lock
   paths with `H.surf_path(h, [(phi, y), ...], out=(root, end))` and `H.fall(...)`.
3. Its bust `*_BODY` (torso/neck tables, cloth, texture, `ribs`, `neckline` or `collar`, `inside` for painted
   clothes, `details` for buttons and edges) and figure `*_FIG` (legs, shoe, skirts, arms, hand, props).
4. Add it to `NAMES`, `LOOK`, `FIG` in `build.py` (a character in a module of its own — as `marat.py`, `kira.py` —
   also to `HOME`). Try a quick head with `draw_head` at yaw 0/35/90 first —
   the turn shows at once whether the shape tables are right.

## Round three: Марат and Кира

What was asked: two more characters in this style, made with its code, next to the four (brief
`docs/briefs/styles-more.md`, part A). What was done:

- `tools/marat.py`, `tools/kira.py` — a module per character: `MARAT` / `KIRA` (head and face), `*_BODY` (bust),
  `*_FIG` (the whole puppet), `EXPR` (their own faces: `beam`, `laugh`; `sulk`, `smirk`). The four stay in `cast.py`.
- `hair.py` got sculpted curls: `curl_spots` (a jittered honeycomb of curl centres over the head, or over a mass that
  leaves the head — `place`, `radius`) and `coils` (each curl a small lit ball with a spiral cut, the whole mass shaded
  as one form). Marat's hair, beard and moustache are made of them — after the clay and yarn hair in the samples
  (`refs/09`, `10`, `11`). The beard hangs from the jaw as a bowl of its own; the moustache's lower edge follows the
  smile of the given face, so the open mouth (teeth, tongue) is never covered. They turn with the head, so his
  turnaround holds in profile.
- Marat's clothes: the apron's bib and straps are painted inside the jacket's form (`inside`), hems and the tie are
  relief (`details`), the towel comes over the shoulder's top; in the figure the apron goes on as a thin loft in front
  of the legs, the sleeves end at the elbow in a roll, the forearms are skin.
- Kira: the fringe is four pointed locks from the part over her left eye; the hood is the `collar` loft with
  `open_neck`; the headphones are drawn in `collar_extra`; in the figure one forearm goes under a "pocket lip" (a
  prop laid over it), the boots are a shaft tube + the shoe + lime laces (a prop).
- `build.py`: the character table takes the module of each (`HOME`); a character may have a pose of its own for the
  full figure (`LOOK[n]['fig']`: expression, turn, `roll`, `head_dx`, `head_dy`, `x`), a resting face for the
  turnaround (`rest`), and its own reading of an expression (`P['faces']`: Kira's joy is the smirk, Marat's the
  laugh). New picture `six` (all six; `cast` — the four — stays). A line of action comes from the torso's `x` table
  (hips aside), the head's roll and shift, and legs that differ.
- By eye: the first moustache was a string of beads and hid the smile — made thicker and lifted with the mouth's
  corners; the beard was a strip along the jaw in profile — carried under the ear; the jacket's collar under the beard
  was a white box — removed; Kira's headphones first looked like two eyes — turned toward each other, the cushion
  showing inside; her portrait was turned the other way so that the fringe falls over the near eye; the hood and the
  hems had thin "wings" at the corners (the smooth outline overshoots a square end) — the lofts now end in a taper.
- The four were not touched: re-rendered with the new code from a copy of `tools/` and compared with `pics/` pixel
  by pixel — identical (14 pictures).

To repeat: `python3 tools/build.py marat`, `python3 tools/build.py kira`, `python3 tools/build.py six`.

Weak: hands are the same mittens (Marat's fist on the ladle is a lump); Kira's hoodie is a smooth box without folds;
Marat's towel is flat; profiles are flat-faced; Marat's anger is the weakest face.

## Honest state

Reads as a puppet on a set; lighting and materials carry it. Faces are simpler than Laika's sculpts, poses are
stiff, hands are mittens, Savely's white hair and moustache look papery, profiles simplify hair and clothes.
